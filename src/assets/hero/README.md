# Hero background clip

Drop the clip here and the Home hero plays it automatically (no code change):

- `hero.mp4`  (required for video): H.264, 1920x1080, 6 to 7 seconds, no sound track needed,
  under about 1.5 MB (the hero loads on mobile data). Seamless loop works best.
- `hero.webm` (optional): same clip as VP9, smaller on Chrome and Android.
- `hero.jpg`  (recommended): the first frame, shown while the video loads and for visitors with
  reduced motion or Data Saver.

Use real footage that AgroStart owns or has written permission to use (everyone shown must have
agreed). Calm, slow camera moves, light and bright, nothing important in the top 40% (the
headline sits there under a white veil).

Until a clip is added, the hero shows an animated scene of crops growing in rows.
